Broadcast Regulations

The Anarchy Broadcasting Authority establishes regulatory standards for digital broadcasting platforms, media organisations, and creators operating within the Anarchy broadcast network.

Regulatory Scope

These regulations apply to any entity distributing digital broadcast content through services or platforms operating within the Anarchy network ecosystem. This includes livestream services, recorded video platforms, and distributed media infrastructure.

Broadcast Operator Requirements

Identification

Broadcast operators must maintain an identifiable account, organisation, or administrative contact responsible for distributed media.

Content Responsibility

Broadcasters are responsible for the content distributed through their channels and must ensure it complies with Authority standards.

Moderation Capability

Interactive broadcasts should implement moderation tools to manage viewer interactions and prevent harmful conduct.

Transparency

Broadcast entities should clearly disclose sponsorships, advertising, or paid promotional content.

Content Restrictions

  • Broadcast content must not promote illegal activities.
  • Material designed to intentionally mislead or manipulate audiences may be restricted.
  • Content promoting harassment, hate campaigns, or targeted abuse is prohibited.
  • Content involving minors must follow strict safety and privacy protections.
  • Creators must respect intellectual property rights.

Platform Responsibilities

Platforms hosting broadcast content within the Anarchy ecosystem should implement moderation systems, reporting mechanisms, and policies aligned with Authority guidelines.

  • Platforms should provide tools for reporting harmful or illegal broadcasts.
  • Platforms should cooperate with Authority investigations where appropriate.
  • Platforms may implement additional safety or community standards.

Compliance Monitoring

The Authority may review broadcasts, platform activity, or creator conduct to ensure compliance with regulatory standards. Monitoring may occur through public reports, automated systems, or manual review processes.

Enforcement Measures

Notice of Concern

Initial warning indicating potential regulatory violations.

Content Restrictions

Temporary removal or restriction of specific broadcast material.

Broadcast Suspension

Temporary suspension of broadcasting privileges.

Network Removal

Permanent removal from the broadcast ecosystem for severe or repeated violations.

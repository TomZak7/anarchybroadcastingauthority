Broadcast Policy Framework

The Anarchy Broadcasting Authority establishes guidelines for digital broadcasting, streaming platforms, creators, and media organisations participating within the Anarchy network ecosystem.

Core Broadcasting Principles

Transparency

Broadcast content must clearly identify the individual, organisation, or entity responsible for publication. Anonymous broadcasts that impersonate others or mislead audiences are prohibited.

Audience Protection

Content creators should consider the safety and wellbeing of viewers, particularly where content may include sensitive, mature, or controversial topics.

Platform Neutrality

Broadcast standards apply regardless of distribution method, including live streams, video platforms, decentralised hosting networks, or embedded media services.

Responsible Speech

Broadcasts must not promote harassment, targeted abuse, coordinated attacks, or harmful misinformation campaigns.

Broadcast Licensing

Organisations or individuals distributing structured programming, livestream channels, or large-scale digital media operations may be required to register as a recognised broadcaster within the Anarchy network.

  • Broadcast entities must provide a verified contact or administrative channel.
  • Creators operating commercial or monetised broadcast channels may require registration.
  • Broadcast operators should maintain moderation capabilities during live programming.
  • Platforms distributing broadcast content should maintain content reporting tools.

Content Classification System

To help audiences understand the nature of broadcast material, the Authority recommends the following classification categories for streaming and recorded media.

G

General audience content suitable for all ages.

T

Content containing mild themes or language intended for teen audiences.

M

Material with mature discussion topics, strong language, or intense themes.

18+

Adult-oriented content that may contain explicit or highly sensitive material.

Creator Compliance

  • Creators should ensure content does not violate intellectual property rights.
  • Live broadcasts should include moderation systems where audience interaction exists.
  • Creators should clearly label sponsored or promotional material.
  • Content involving minors must follow strict safety and privacy protections.
  • Creators must respond reasonably to legitimate content complaints.

Complaints & Investigations

Members of the public may submit complaints regarding broadcasts believed to violate policy guidelines. Complaints may be reviewed by the Authority where appropriate.

  • Complaints should include the broadcast source and timestamp where possible.
  • The Authority may request clarification from the broadcaster or platform.
  • Content may be temporarily restricted during investigation.
  • Repeat violations may result in broadcaster warnings or platform restrictions.

Enforcement Measures

Where broadcasters repeatedly violate policy guidelines, the Authority may recommend the following enforcement measures.

Advisory Notice

Initial warning issued to a broadcaster identifying potential policy violations.

Content Restrictions

Temporary limitation of broadcast visibility or platform distribution.

Broadcast Suspension

Temporary suspension from the broadcast network for severe or repeated violations.

Network Ban

Permanent removal from the Anarchy broadcast ecosystem.
